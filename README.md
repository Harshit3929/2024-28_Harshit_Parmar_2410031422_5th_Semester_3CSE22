## 📌 About the Internship

The internship focused on building a strong practical foundation in **concrete technology and industrial quality control**, applied to live ready-mix concrete production — raw material validation, mix design, batching, and verification of fresh and hardened concrete. It followed a progressive, phase-wise structure combining plant orientation, laboratory work, and live production observation, culminating in the maintenance of quality control records that make each production batch traceable.

**Organization:** Sunshine Readymix — a Ready-Mix Concrete producer operating a batching plant at Rithoj Road, Village Bhondsi, Haryana – 122102. Its R&D department is responsible for developing and validating concrete mix designs, monitoring incoming raw material quality, and maintaining consistency across production batches.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop a working understanding of the constituent materials of concrete — cement, coarse and fine aggregates, water, and chemical admixtures
- Study the proportioning of concrete mixes for target grades, and how trial mixes are adjusted to meet strength and workability requirements
- Observe and support the quality assurance procedures applied at every stage of production
- Gain practical exposure to standard laboratory tests on raw materials and on fresh and hardened concrete
- Understand the end-to-end operation of an RMC plant, from raw material storage and batching through mixing, transport, and delivery
- Appreciate the quality control documentation that makes each production batch traceable and auditable
